# Herbivore

Keiji is twenty-four, a civil engineering student at the University of Tokyo, and by all appearances, a quiet young man drifting through life. He skips lectures, buys skincare products, and reads magazines he doesn't need. His father just lost his job. His only real friend doesn't know he's being used.

Beneath the surface of modern Japanese society — its crushing commuter trains, its bright screens, its polite silences — something is building. Keiji belongs to a generation labeled "herbivore men": passive, gentle, unambitious. But what happens when all that stillness turns out to be preparation?

A story that explores the tensions simmering in modern Japanese society — between tradition and withdrawal, expectation and refusal, gentleness and violence — building to a conclusion that will leave you breathless.
