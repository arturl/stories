# A Note on Authorship

*The Dogs of Kolkata* was written by Artur Laksberg.

All prose in the novel is the author's own.

Claude (Anthropic) assisted with the following:
- Historical research (the 1947 Partition, the 1971 Liberation War, MSF history,
  the geography and culture of Kolkata and Dhaka)
- Plot structure and timeline
- Character development through dialogue and discussion
- Tracking open issues and unresolved plot lines
- Editorial feedback on style and prose

The author read widely, made all creative decisions, and wrote all the words.
