# The Dogs Of Kolkata — Open Issues

## Unresolved Plot Lines

- What specifically does Farah build between 1947 and 1971?
  (a school, a community network, something connected to the dogs?)
- Does Farah marry? Does she have children?
- How does Nikhil learn she is alive before coming to find her?
- Does Nikhil come to Bangladesh deliberately to find her,
  or does he arrive for other reasons and seek her out?

## Open Questions — Character

- What does Farah feel when Nikhil arrives — joy, resentment, indifference, all three?
- Does she tell him what 1971 cost her? Does she tell him about Kamal's origins?
- Does he try to give her money or help? Does she refuse?
- What does he understand at the end that he did not when he arrived?

## Open Questions — Kamal and Nikhil

- How does Kamal's first scene with Nikhil play out physically — where are they,
  what is the setting, who speaks first?
- How long does Nikhil stay? Days? Weeks? The length of his stay
  affects how much the Kamal relationship can develop.
- Does Kamal ask Nikhil directly about 1971 — were you there? Did you look for her?
  This could be the scene where the near-miss is finally spoken aloud.
- Does Kamal come to see Nikhil as any kind of father figure, even partially?
  Or does the respect stay professional — two men who do good work, differently?
- The water conversation: where does it happen? Evening, outside?
  Is a dog present during this specific exchange?
- What does Nikhil offer Kamal, practically? Connections, funding for a project,
  a letter of introduction? Does Kamal accept or refuse?

## Open Questions — The Ending

- Does Nikhil leave Bangladesh at the end, or does he stay?
- If he leaves — what is the last image? Him going, or Farah and Kamal after?
- The dog in the final scene should not be introduced for the first time there —
  it needs to have appeared earlier in the Dhaka sections so its presence
  at the end feels earned, not placed.
- The hope in the ending lives in Kamal and his rivers. How do we see this
  without stating it? A drawing, a map, a conversation he has with someone
  about a project? Something concrete and visual.

## Open Questions — The Dogs

- What is the name or identity of the specific dog in the Sutanuti scene?
  Does this dog, or a descendant, reappear?
- How do the Dhaka dogs differ from the Kolkata dogs — culturally, visually?
- What role does a dog play in the reunion scene?

## Ideas to Consider

- Nikhil was in Dhaka in 1971 with a medical team and did not find her.
  Does Farah ever learn this? When? How does she receive it?
- The poster on the wall in the opening scene — what does it say?
  Muslim League? Congress? Worth researching the specific political moment,
  June 1946, to find the right poster.
