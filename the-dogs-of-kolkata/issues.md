# The Dogs Of Kolkata — Open Issues

## Unresolved Plot Lines

- What specifically does Farah build between 1952 and 1971?
  (a school, a community network, something connected to the dogs?)
  This is the moral spine of the middle section and needs to be specific.
- Who is Farah's husband? He needs a name and enough presence to be mourned.
  Not a major character but a real one.
- How does Nikhil find Farah in 2000? Does he know her address?
  Does he ask around the neighborhood? Does someone lead him to her?
- Is Nikhil's wife alive in 2000? Dead? Does Farah know about her?

## Open Questions — Sami

- Sami must be real before she disappears. What scenes show her?
  Her relationship with Farah. Her relationship with dogs.
  Something she does that is hers alone.
- Does Farah ever speak of Sami to Kamal? What does she call her —
  Samara or Sami? The daak naam is the intimate name. When does she use it?
- Kamal grew up with a ghost — a sister who existed before him.
  Does he feel like a replacement? This is delicate and worth exploring.

## Open Questions — Nikhil

- Who is Nikhil's wife? Does she appear in the novel or only as a presence?
- Does Nikhil tell anyone the truth about 1971, ever?
  Or does he carry it entirely alone until he dies?
- What does Nikhil's life look like from the outside in 2000 —
  successful, respected — versus what it feels like from the inside?

## Open Questions — Kamal and Nikhil

- How does Kamal's first scene with Nikhil play out physically?
  Where are they, what is the setting, who speaks first?
- How long does Nikhil stay? Days? Weeks?
  The length of his stay shapes how much the relationship can develop.
- Does Kamal ever ask directly: were you in Dhaka in 1971?
  If he does — and Nikhil answers honestly — what happens to the
  relationship they have built?
- What does Nikhil offer Kamal practically — connections, a letter,
  funding for a project? Does Kamal accept?

## Open Questions — The Ending

- What is the last image of the novel?
  Nikhil leaving, or Farah and Kamal after he has gone?
- The dog in the final scene must appear earlier in the Dhaka sections —
  it needs to be known before it matters.
- The hope lives in Kamal and his rivers. How is this shown without stating it?
  A drawing, a map, a conversation about a specific project?

## Open Questions — The Dogs

- Does the Sutanuti dog, or a descendant, reappear in Dhaka?
- How do the Dhaka dogs differ from the Kolkata dogs — in how they are
  treated, in how Farah relates to them in a Muslim community?
- What specific dog is present at the reunion, and when did we first meet it?

## Structural Questions

- The 1971 section is the gut punch. How long is it?
  It cannot be summarized. The reader must live it.
  Sami's last ordinary day. The husband not coming home.
  The specific sound of the city the night Operation Searchlight begins.
- The novel has two zeroes — Partition and 1971. The second building
  must feel different from the first: harder, older, from further down.
  How do we show that difference?

## Ideas to Consider

- The poster on the wall in the opening scene — research the specific
  political moment of June 1946 to confirm Muslim League poster is accurate.
  What specifically would it have said or advertised?
- The Language Movement of 1952: does Farah lose someone she knows?
  A neighbor, a young man from her street? A specific loss here would
  connect her to the political history personally.
- Nikhil's cholera work and Kamal's river engineering: research the
  specific cholera outbreaks in Bangladesh and the major flood events
  to ground their conversation in real technical detail.
