# The Last Call

Ray Tillman used to be a good cop — until a pill addiction and a terrible accident destroyed everything. Now he's a social worker, four years clean, trying to make amends one child at a time. When a routine welfare check on a nine-year-old boy leads him into the crosshairs of a brutal trafficking ring, Ray is pulled back into the world he left behind.

Standing beside him is Lieutenant Sofia Martinez, a sharp and relentless officer who keeps telling him to stay behind the line — and who keeps watching him cross it. As the case escalates from a run-down duplex to a full-scale raid, Ray must decide how far he's willing to go to save the children no one else is fighting for, even if it costs him the second chance he's only just begun to build.
